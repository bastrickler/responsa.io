Turn a labor-hour task order into a scoped deliverable — before it goes out the door.

Responsa is an early prototype tool for federal program offices and contracting officers. Paste in a task order and it separates the work into pieces, recommends a firm-fixed-price deliverable or a level-of-effort structure for each, and flags what's missing before you take it to your CO.

How it works

No forms to fill out — just describe the work in your own words.

  1. Paste in your task order. A rough draft, an excerpt, or a description in plain language — whatever you have.
  2. It's separated into distinct pieces of work. Real task orders usually bundle several different kinds of work under one title.
  3. Each piece gets a plain-language recommendation. A defined deliverable, a capped level of effort, or — rarely — a specific reason it may still need to stay time-and-materials.
  4. Anything missing gets flagged. Before the draft goes anywhere, you'll see what still needs an answer.

This is an early prototype. We're testing it with a small number of program offices before it's more broadly available.

Nothing produced here is a contract-type determination — that's always your contracting officer's call. This is meant to give you a solid first draft to bring to that conversation.
