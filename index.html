Three outcomes, never a price

Every position lands in one of three contract types. Responsa recommends structure — deliverables, effort, or the rare justified exception — never a rate.

FFP

Firm-Fixed-Price

When the output can be defined well enough to check as done or not — a real deliverable with a real acceptance criterion, not one manufactured to look tidy.

FPLOE

Fixed-Price, Level-of-Effort

When the work is real and ongoing but its output can't be fully defined in advance — presence, expressed as a fraction of one full-time role, not raw hours that can drift.

T&M

Time-and-Materials

Only when neither the output nor the extent of the work can be estimated with real confidence. FAR 16.601 treats this as the exception — so does Responsa.

Example
"Cyber Defense Analyst, Level II, IAW PWS 3.2.1 — monitors network alerts, escalates anomalies, and maintains monthly compliance documentation."
CLIN 0001 · FPLOE

Reviews incoming network alerts and escalates anything abnormal. fte_fraction 0.75 of one full-time position, effort verified by activity logs — not by results.

CLIN 0002 · FFP (companion, genuine)

Monthly compliance report, accepted against a defined checklist — a real deliverable already implied by the duties, not one added for appearances.

No price appears anywhere in this output. That's set later, by competitive quotes among vetted candidates.

How we're different from a staffing prime

Responsa is a pre-award vetting and matchmaking layer — not a brokerage, not an employer of record, and never a party to the award.

1

We never set a price

Every CLIN Responsa drafts describes structure — a deliverable or a level of effort — never a rate or dollar figure. Pricing is set later, by competitive quotes among vetted candidates.

2

We don't manufacture deliverables

Most ongoing roles are honestly FPLOE, full stop. We only add a companion FFP deliverable when one is genuinely implied by the work — padding one on to look different from a prime is worse than not having one.

3

We stay pre-award

Responsa scopes the requirement and surfaces a vetted shortlist. The CO awards directly to the consultant or microbusiness — no vehicle, no intermediary, no cut of what the contractor is paid.
