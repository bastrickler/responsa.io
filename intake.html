Send us a rough need

Tell us what your office is trying to get done. We’ll return a scoped version, and you can correct it instead of writing it from scratch.

What you get back

  • A problem statement and the decision it supports
  • The work separated by type, so each piece can be priced and accepted on its own terms
  • Milestones with acceptance criteria
  • An effort range, with the reasoning shown
  • A list of what your office provides, and by when
Before you write Please don’t include classified information, CUI, export-controlled data or source-selection-sensitive material. Describe the need in general terms. We can fill in details later through an approved channel.

What happens next

An expert reviews your submission and drafts the scope. We don’t share your submission with any expert without your approval.
